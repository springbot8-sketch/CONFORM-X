Patch array

Four thin, flexible microstrip patches at half-wavelength spacing. Carries UHF to the handheld radio and L-band (1.28 GHz) for body-cam video and audio.

Flexible substrate

Ultra-thin low-loss laminate. Sets patch size and bandwidth while bending with the helmet curve.

Ground plane

Copper layer that reflects energy upward and outward and gives the patches their return path.

RF shield

Absorptive underside layer isolates the wearer's head (SAR) and pushes the pattern up and away. Sits on the Kevlar shell without altering its ballistic rating.
